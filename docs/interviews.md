# Interviews

The brief asks: "How do you know it's real? Name at least one person you spoke to." Each teammate adds one row by the end of Day 1.

Ask permission to use their first name and quote in the pitch. First name only, nothing else that identifies them.

## Four questions

1. Has anyone tried to scam you or your parent? What happened?
2. What did you do in the first five minutes?
3. Would you have asked family first? Why or why not?
4. What would stop you from using an app like this?

## What we changed because of it

- Both scams used the child as bait, so the pitch leads with it: "The scam uses the child. Our app asks the real one." The app sends the parent's question to their real adult child, the helper.
- Alex's mother got a phone call, not a text. The app only checks pasted messages, so phone calls are listed in the pitch under what we still need to learn.
- The demo helper should not be called Alex, so judges don't mix them up with the Alex we interviewed.
